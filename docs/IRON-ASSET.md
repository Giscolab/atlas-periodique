## Prompt final

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: standalone photorealistic illustration of an iron specimen for a dark museum-style element detail card, UI will label it Illustration separately. Generate one image, square 1:1 composition. A single small irregular chunk of iron metal, dark steel gray with natural rough fractured facets, fine pitting and restrained satin silver highlights. Three-quarter view, centered, the object occupies about 72 percent of the frame. Photorealistic studio macro specimen photography; physically plausible metallic iron surface, not a gem or polished mirror. Soft key light from upper left and a faint rim on right. Genuinely transparent background with preserved alpha, no backdrop or floor. No text, letters, symbols, labels, hands, pedestal, supporting objects, glow, sparks or watermark. Only the isolated iron chunk.
